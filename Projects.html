YourTaste | AI-Powered Food Discovery

Personal Project · Founder & Developer · Fall 2025

YourTaste is an AI-powered platform I independently developed to simplify plant-based living by bringing restaurant discovery, personalized recipe generation, and meal planning together in one application. The idea came from my own experience trying to find restaurants that accommodate vegetarian diets and figuring out what to cook using ingredients already available at home. I wanted to create a platform that made both processes easier and more convenient.

The platform supports personalized restaurant recommendations based on dietary preferences, location, cuisine, and other filters. It also includes an AI-powered assistant called VegBot, which helps users explore food options, receive suggestions, and find restaurants that fit their needs.

AI Recipe Generation & Ingredient Scanning

The recipe generation feature allows users to provide ingredients they already have and receive personalized recipe suggestions. I integrated the Gemini API to process ingredient information and generate recipes while accounting for dietary restrictions and preferences. The ingredient scanner also provides an interface for identifying ingredients through photos, image uploads, and barcodes.

Restaurant Discovery & Personalization

I integrated the Google Maps API to support location-based restaurant discovery. Users can search by location and apply filters for dietary preferences such as vegetarian, vegan, Jain, kosher, and gluten-free. These features make it easier to find restaurants that align with individual dietary requirements.

  • Independently designed and developed the application using Lovable as the development foundation.
  • Integrated Gemini API for AI-powered recipe generation and personalized food suggestions.
  • Connected Google Maps API to enable location-based restaurant discovery and filtering.
  • Developed personalized dietary filters, chatbot functionality, and meal-planning features.
  • Worked with Supabase to support application data and backend functionality.
  • Grew the platform to 50+ users and received international hackathon recognition.

Spydr | AI Knowledge Graph & Research Platform

Professional Project · AI Research & Product Development

Spydr is an AI research platform designed to organize, connect, and retrieve information through an interconnected knowledge graph. The platform transforms information into structured networks of entities, concepts, and relationships. Rather than treating information as isolated documents, users can explore connections between different topics through an interactive graph.

Knowledge Graph Exploration

The knowledge graph interface represents information through nodes and edges. Individual nodes represent entities such as people, organizations, technologies, and concepts, while connections illustrate relationships between them. This allows users to visually explore how different pieces of information relate to one another and navigate complex collections of knowledge.

Deep Research & Backend Development

During my internship as an AI Research & Product Development Intern, I contributed to Spydr's Deep Research system. My work focused on improving how user queries were refined, broken into smaller research questions, and processed into structured results.

I worked on prompt refinement, sub-query generation, keyword-injection logic, and JSON output formatting to improve retrieval accuracy. I also contributed to backend functionality, including rate limiting and token management, and helped integrate FastAPI with Neo4j to support the platform's knowledge graph infrastructure.

My Technical Contributions

  • Refined research prompts and developed sub-query generation logic for the Deep Research AI product.
  • Implemented keyword-injection techniques to improve information retrieval and research relevance.
  • Tuned structured JSON outputs to improve consistency between AI-generated results and backend processing.
  • Implemented rate limiting and token management to support controlled and scalable system usage.
  • Contributed to backend and graph database integration using FastAPI and Neo4j.
  • Tested the system across 10+ domains and contributed to a platform serving 300+ users.
